Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Yard Dog is a game made by CW Games ("we"). This policy covers the Yard Dog website, the web version of the game, and the Yard Dog app for Android.

What we collect

We don't collect personal information. Yard Dog has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Your game save is stored only on your own device (in your browser's local storage, or inside the app). It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes it. You can export a backup copy yourself from the game's Settings.

In-app purchases (Android app)

The Android app offers optional purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To confirm and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-processing service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (which items you bought and when), and uses it only to make purchases work. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Website hosting

The company that hosts this website may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser type, for security and reliability. We don't use these logs to identify you.

Children

Yard Dog is intended for players 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
